Browse the live weekly menu, choose Lean or Bulk for each dish, select DFW delivery or free Frisco pickup, and submit your order online. You receive an itemized email copy with Zelle instructions for payments@getprpd.com, then Rida personally confirms payment and fulfillment details.

If you need help choosing meals or want a plan built around a specific goal, use the custom-plan intake form and Rida will text you directly.

Customization & Macros

Lean is the lighter portion, while Bulk provides a larger serving with more calories and protein. Every dish shows its current portion macros and price before you add it, and you can order both tiers of the same dish.

Macros are calculated from measured recipe ingredients and portion weights. They are practical preparation estimates rather than laboratory-tested values, and each weekly dish displays its current calories and protein before you order.

Delivery & Pricing

We currently deliver across the DFW metroplex, including Frisco, Plano, Allen, McKinney, Irving, Carrollton, Richardson, Garland, and surrounding areas. Not sure if we cover your area? Message us on Instagram @getprpd and we'll let you know.

Every dish and tier is priced on the live menu. Your delivery ZIP determines the applicable area. Local delivery has a $60 order minimum, a $9.99 fee, and free delivery at an $85 food subtotal. Regional delivery has an $80 order minimum, a $12.99 fee, and free delivery at a $125 food subtotal. Extended delivery has a $100 order minimum, a $14.99 fee, and free delivery at a $150 food subtotal. Checkout shows your exact policy and total before submission.

No. The Local Delivery order minimum is $60. A local food subtotal of $85 unlocks free delivery; orders from $60 through $84.99 can still be placed and have the standard $9.99 local delivery fee. Regional and Extended Delivery have their own ZIP-based minimums and free-delivery thresholds, all shown before you submit the order.

The website records your order but does not charge a card. Your itemized email directs Zelle payment to payments@getprpd.com, and Rida texts you to confirm payment and delivery. Confirm that Zelle displays PRPD before sending; your order is not considered paid until Rida confirms it.

If your bank blocks the new business recipient, do not repeatedly retry the transfer. Reply to your order email or text Rida for an alternative payment option so your order can be handled safely.

PRPD is running a manually approved referral pilot. Paid PRPD customers can request a personal referral code. A new customer receives $10 off their first qualifying order, and the referring customer earns a $10 credit after that referred order is paid. Credits are not cash, cannot be combined on the same order, and are confirmed by Rida before use.

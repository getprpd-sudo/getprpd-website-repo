# PRPD Batch Production Record

Updated: July 20, 2026

Use one copy per delivery batch. Planning values come from the Cook-Day Planner; this record contains only measured actuals and documented deviations. Blank means unknown. Never replace a missing measurement with an estimate.

This is intentionally smaller than a restaurant HACCP binder. It captures the evidence PRPD needs to reproduce recipes, prevent shortages, protect food safety, verify labels, and understand cost.

## 6. Quality And Customer Readiness

For a new or modified recipe, check taste immediately, after refrigeration, and after the customer reheating method.

| Dish | Fresh taste / texture | Cold day checked | Reheat method / result | Appearance / container fit | Freeze suitability | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | |

Decision must be one of:

- Retest: recipe changed or failed yield/quality.
- Provisional: one successful controlled run.
- Approved: repeated successfully using the same ingredients, method, equipment, portion, and yield.
- Retired: removed from production until rebuilt.

PRPD operating rule: treat three consistent controlled runs as strong approval evidence. This is an internal quality rule, not a regulatory threshold.

## Minimum Required Record

When cook day is too busy, capture these ten items before anything optional:

1. Final planned customer count by dish/tier.
2. Actual prepared, packed, leftover, and wasted counts.
3. Raw and cooked usable protein weights.
4. Dry and cooked rice weights.
5. Actual portion spot-checks for the first, middle, and final container.
6. Animal-protein endpoint temperatures.
7. Cooling times and temperatures for cooked TCS food.
8. Every ingredient, method, or portion deviation.
9. Start/finish times and the main equipment bottleneck.
10. Fresh, refrigerated, and reheated quality result for every new or modified dish.

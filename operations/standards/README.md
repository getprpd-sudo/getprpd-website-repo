# Standards And Rules

These documents define repeatable operating rules and templates. They are not batch-specific.

- `RECIPE_DATA_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` - recipe authority and approval status.
- `FULL_RECIPE_LIBRARY_INDEX.md` - complete recipe inventory.
- `MACRO_VERIFICATION_PROTOCOL.md` - nutrition-calculation rules.
- `STANDARD_RECIPE_TEMPLATE.md` - controlled recipe format.
- `COOK_DAY_CHECKLIST.md` - required production workflow.
- `BATCH_PRODUCTION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md` - actual-yield and quality record.
- `WEEKLY_CLOSEOUT_TEMPLATE.md` - finance and operations closeout.
- `WEEKLY_MENU_ROLLOVER.md` - menu transition procedure.
- `WEEKLY_SIDE_SYSTEM.md` - side and freezer-suitability rules.

Do not replace these standards with chat estimates or one-time batch notes.

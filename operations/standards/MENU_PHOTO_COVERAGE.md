### Owner-requested sauce pouring capture, October 19

Capture each actual finished weekly sauce, not a stand-in. Use the real portion cup and a plain or accurately PRPD-labeled squeeze bottle containing that sauce. Keep the bottle tip/body, uninterrupted stream and cup rim/base visible together. Do not use the Sriracha manufacturer bottle to imply that the blended Sweet Heat is plain Sriracha.

- Still: landscape 4:3, three-quarter angle, neutral uncluttered background, soft side/window light. Leave framing room around the bottle and cup. Take a sharp mid-pour frame plus a clean filled-cup hero and sealed-cup reference. No vertical menu-card image.
- Clip: landscape 16:9, camera fixed, approximately 6-8 seconds per take. Hold 1-2 seconds before the pour and after stopping. Keep all important content inside a central 4:3-safe crop. Stop at the real portion fill; do not overflow for the shot.
- Initial web treatment: use the real still as the card image. Review the clip for an optional short motion treatment after capture, with a still fallback. A filling cup does not naturally form a seamless loop because the fill level resets; do not promise an endless seamless pour from one take.
- New photos/clips are owner-planned, not received or published. Do not replace the concept label until actual product media is inspected and assigned.

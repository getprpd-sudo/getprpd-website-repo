# PRPD Component Meal Portion Standard

Effective October 7, 2026. Owner approved after the Sweet Chili chicken and Boy Kibble plating tests.

## Controlled baseline

| Component | Lean | Bulk | Control basis |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Boneless skinless chicken thigh | 160 g cooked | 210 g cooked | Weigh before sauce |
| Chicken raw pull | 232.5581 g | 305.2326 g | Current one-run yield: 430 g cooked / 625 g raw = 68.8% |
| 90/10 ground beef | 210 g raw | 300 g raw | Do not replace with drained 80/20 |
| Finished seasoned beef component | 175 g working target | 250 g working target | Hold until an actual 90/10 yield is measured |
| Cooked starch | 120 g | 160 g | Plated target |
| Basmati rice | 45 g dry | 60 g dry | Controlled equivalent pending measured cooked-rice yield |
| Vegetables | 75 g | 100 g | Baseline side; named recipes may exceed it |

The finished plated protein and starch weights control pack-out. Raw purchasing reverses the current applicable yield, then receives the separate purchasing reserve. Sauce, cheese and named toppings are recipe-specific and do not replace the protein, starch or vegetable controls.

The 68.8% chicken yield is one measured pan, not a permanent universal yield. Replace it only with a documented rolling yield from compatible boneless-thigh cooks. It does not apply to breast, mince, bone-in chicken, seafood, steak or format-specific recipes.

## Direct standard builds

These recipes use the 160/210 g cooked thigh or 210/300 g raw 90/10 beef control plus the 45/60 g dry basmati control. Recipe-specific vegetables may exceed the 75/100 g baseline when they define the dish.

| Family | Meals |
|---|---|
| Chicken | Sweet Chili Chicken with Rice; Harissa Honey Chicken; Halal Cart Chicken + Yellow Rice; Mexican Streetcorn Chicken Bowl; Mediterranean Chicken Bowl; Chinese-Style Bok Choy Chicken |
| Ground beef | Boy Kibble; Southwest Beef Taco Bowl |

## Translated standard builds

The protein control applies now. Their non-rice starch remains recipe-specific until its cooked yield is measured and translated to the 120/160 g plated target.

| Meal | Translation hold |
|---|---|
| Power Bowl | Measure cooked potato yield and complete vegetable/cheese assembly |
| Loaded Buffalo Chicken Potato | Measure the cooked potato allocation |
| Beef Breakfast Skillet | Measure cooked potato yield and complete vegetable assembly |

## True format or protein exceptions

Do not force the blanket grams into these recipes. Their own count, structure, protein identity or incorporated yield controls the build.

- Incorporated rice or sauced dishes: Chicken Biryani and Butter Chicken.
- Pasta, mac, casserole and layered dishes: BBQ Chicken Mac & Cheese, Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta, Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta, Creamy Chicken Parmesan, Chicken Enchilada Bake, Loaded Beef Cottage Pie and High-Protein Beef Lasagna.
- Wraps, tacos, quesadillas, burritos, sliders, flatbreads, pinwheels and hot pockets.
- Meatballs, seekh, shawarma and kofta.
- Steak and beef-strip meals, including Pepper Steak and Bulgogi.
- Bone-in chicken, including Peri Peri and Tandoori.
- Seafood meals.
- Grab & Go boxes and fixed single-size products.
- Chicken Shakshuka and other mixed-protein or egg-centered composed dishes.
- Custom chicken breast or mince meals.
- Nouman's kofta remains actual 330 g raw 96/4 beef. It never inherits the 90/10 standard.

## Release holds

- Record at least the next compatible boneless-thigh raw and cooked batch weights before replacing the one-run 68.8% yield with a rolling value.
- Record dry and finished cooked basmati weights before replacing the 45/60 g dry control with a final cooked-rice conversion.
- Record an actual 90/10 raw, cooked/drained and finished seasoned-component yield before treating 175/250 g as anything beyond a working plated target.
- Recalculate customer macros and direct cost whenever a controlled portion changes. Do not copy macros between recipe families.

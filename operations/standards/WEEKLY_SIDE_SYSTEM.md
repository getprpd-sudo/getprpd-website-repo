# PRPD Weekly Side System

Status: Proposed for this week's production test

## Goal

Make meals look complete and add vegetables or fiber without creating a different prep project for every menu item. Use three shared side batches across the menu, then calculate macros only after the plated gram targets are approved.

## Side A - Breakfast Hash

Use the same hash already produced for Egg Bites.

Starting plated target:

- Lean side: 75g cooked
- Bulk side: 100g cooked

Best matches:

- Breakfast Quesadilla
- Grilled Cheese Breakfast Burrito

Egg Bites already use their confirmed 120g Lean / 150g Bulk hash portions and should not be changed by this document.

## Side B - Roasted Green Vegetables

Use one batch of broccoli and green beans rather than separate recipes.

### Starting Batch Formula

Per 1,000g raw vegetables:

- Broccoli florets: 600g
- Trimmed green beans: 400g
- Avocado oil: 20g
- Garlic powder: 4g
- Black pepper: 2g
- Salt: start with 8g and adjust after tasting
- Lemon juice after roasting: 15g

Roast hot enough to brown without making the vegetables soft. For meals intended for freezing, slightly undercook the vegetables so reheating finishes them instead of turning them mushy.

Starting plated target:

- Lean side: 70g cooked
- Bulk side: 90g cooked

Best matches:

- Butter Chicken
- Peri Peri Chicken
- Meatball Arrabbiata Pasta

Do not add this side to Loaded Buffalo Chicken Potato because that meal already includes broccoli. Mexican Streetcorn Chicken already has a full corn component.

## Side C - Pickled Vegetable Mix

Buying a consistent halal-compatible commercial product is acceptable for this week and is more efficient than creating a new pickling process immediately. Record the brand and nutrition label.

Recommended mixture:

- Pickled turnips
- Pickled cucumber
- Pickled red onion

Drain thoroughly before weighing and packing.

Starting plated target:

- Inside Lean shawarma: 30g
- Inside Bulk shawarma: 30g
- Beside Bulk extra seekh portion: additional 15g
- Other meals using it as a side: 30-40g

Best matches:

- Beef Seekh Kabab Shawarma
- Halal Cart Chicken + Yellow Rice
- Boy Kibble

Keep pickled vegetables separate from meals that customers are instructed to freeze. Lettuce and creamy sauces should also remain separate because their thawed quality is poor.

## Meals That Already Have a Complete Side Structure

- Egg Bites: potato hash
- French Toast: strawberries, banana, and whipped cream
- Halal Cart Chicken: yellow rice, fresh vegetables, sauce, plus optional pickles
- Loaded Buffalo Chicken Potato: potato and broccoli
- Mexican Streetcorn Chicken Bowl: rice and street corn
- Boy Kibble: rice and fresh toppings, plus optional pickles

Do not add food merely to make every tray look identical. A side should improve the meal's flavor, appearance, nutrition, or reheating experience.

## Production Rule

1. Count the number of Lean and Bulk meals assigned to each side.
2. Multiply by the plated gram target.
3. Add a 5% production buffer, not an arbitrary extra pan.
4. Record raw weight, cooked yield, and unused remainder.
5. Change the published macros only after the actual finished side portions are confirmed.

## Food Quality Note

Frozen safety and frozen quality are different. Freeze meals promptly while they are still at peak quality. Use roasted vegetables for freezer-intended meals and keep lettuce, creamy sauces, and pickled garnishes separate where possible.

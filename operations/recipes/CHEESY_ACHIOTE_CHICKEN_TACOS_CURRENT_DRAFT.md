## Inspiration boundary

The correct restaurant reference is Talkin' Tacos, whose Plano location serves Frisco and The Colony. Its public menu describes Chicken Achiote tacos with corn tortillas, achiote chicken thigh, avocado salsa, pickled onions, Cotija and cilantro. Its Southwest Chicken tacos demonstrate the restaurant's more layered approach through black-bean salsa, spicy mayo, fried onions, Cotija and cilantro. The menu also uses salsa verde on other tacos and separately sells jalapeno, cayenne and habanero hot sauces. It does not disclose a second proprietary sauce or a formula for the Chicken Achiote taco.

PRPD should borrow the layering principle, color and contrast rather than claim or copy the restaurant's recipe. The public menu name should not use the restaurant's name.

## Recommended PRPD direction

**Cheesy Achiote Chicken Tacos**

- Corn tortillas rather than the previous generic high-fiber flour-tortilla assumption.
- Boneless skinless chicken thighs in a PRPD-developed achiote, citrus, garlic and spice marinade.
- A measured layer of melted Chihuahua or Monterey Jack-style cheese to preserve the original Cheesy Chicken Tacos identity.
- Pickled red onion for acidity and color.
- Cotija for a salty finish rather than using more melting cheese.
- Cilantro.
- One measured chilled avocado-lime Greek-yogurt crema.
- One separate measured PRPD Spicy Chile-Lime Sauce, developed as a house adaptation rather than represented as Talkin' Tacos' formula.

This direction is preferable to copying the restaurant's Southwest Chicken taco because the selected weekly menu already includes Mexican Streetcorn Chicken. Adding black-bean salsa, corn and fried onions to the taco would make the two meals overlap too heavily.

## Proposed meal architecture

| Tier | Taco count | Initial portion direction |
|---|---:|---|
| Lean | 3 tacos | Three complete medium corn-tortilla tacos with measured chicken, melting cheese and garnish |
| Bulk | 4 tacos | Four complete tacos using the same per-taco build |

Use one standardized taco unit so both tiers scale by physical count. Final raw chicken, cheese, Cotija, onion and crema weights remain open until the first fit and reheat test.

The three/four count is appropriate only with a true street-size corn tortilla. Do not use the current Mission Carb Balance medium soft-taco tortilla for this count.

Do not default to Mission Zero Net Carbs or Carb Balance street-taco flour tortillas. Three Mission Zero Net Carb street tortillas provide 19 g labeled fiber before the rest of the meal, while three Carb Balance Whole Wheat street tortillas provide 30 g. That much added-fiber tortilla can dominate the meal's texture and nutrition profile without making it taste more like the corn tacos that inspired this recipe.

Initial decision rule: buy the Mission White Corn Street Taco 24-count package first. It is the closest readily available Walmart match to the restaurant-style 3-taco architecture. Send or record the physical front and nutrition panels before final macros. Test La Banderita Blue Corn only if its flavor is noticeably better and the blue color fits the intended presentation.

## Avocado finish

Sealing plain avocado slows oxygen exposure but does not guarantee that it will stay green. Browning is mainly a quality and appearance problem, and browned avocado can still be edible, but it is not an acceptable customer presentation.

Use a separate chilled sauce cup rather than putting avocado directly inside the reheated taco. The first test should use a blended avocado-lime Greek-yogurt crema with minimal air incorporated. Fill the cup fully enough to minimize headspace, seal immediately and hold at 40 F or below. Compare color, separation, aroma and taste at 24, 48 and 72 hours. If the 72-hour appearance fails, replace it with a tomatillo-jalapeno yogurt salsa and keep avocado out of the production recipe.

## Second sauce

The complete customer build receives a second cup: **PRPD Spicy Chile-Lime Sauce**. Talkin' Tacos lists spicy mayo on its Southwest Chicken and Bang Bang Shrimp tacos, while its Chicken Achiote taco itself lists only avocado salsa. This sauce is therefore a PRPD adaptation that adds a warm chile finish without claiming to reproduce the restaurant's recipe.

First 30 g bench test:

- 21 g light mayonnaise
- 6 g Valentina Black Label hot sauce
- 3 g lime juice

Whisk cold, rest refrigerated for at least 30 minutes, then score heat, salt, acidity and pairing with the achiote chicken. If it tastes flat, adjust only through a separately recorded second test. Do not quietly change the production ratio. Pack one 30 g cup per meal unless the physical taste test supports a smaller fill.

Wash and dry the whole avocado before cutting so the knife does not carry contamination from the peel into the flesh.

## Packaging test

Test two versions side by side:

1. **Owner-preferred assembled taco:** chicken and melting cheese remain inside the corn tortillas. Pickled onion, Cotija and cilantro may remain on the taco only if the hold test passes. Avocado crema and Spicy Chile-Lime Sauce stay in two separate cold cups.
2. **Fallback taco kit:** hot chicken-and-cheese filling in the main compartment, tortillas wrapped separately and the same two cold sauce cups. Use this only if assembled tortillas fail the hold test.

Choose the version that performs better after a 48- and 72-hour refrigerated hold and microwave reheat. Do not describe it as crispy unless the held customer version actually stays crisp.

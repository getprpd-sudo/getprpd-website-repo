# Strawberry Cheesecake Adaptation

Status: Controlled one-cup taste test ready

## Confirmed Direction

The original one-cup formula is now confirmed. This test adds one-third scoop of vanilla protein powder to improve protein while limiting the risk of a chalky filling.

## One-Cup Test Recipe

### Crust

- Ladyfinger cookies, 26g, finely crushed
- Butter, 4g, melted
- Water or unsweetened almond milk, start with 4g and use no more than 6g total

### Filling

- Philadelphia No-Bake Filling, 32g
- Simple Truth Nonfat Greek Yogurt, 70g
- Transparent Labs vanilla protein powder, 11.6g, equal to one-third of a 34.7g scoop
- Monk fruit sweetener, 14g
- Vanilla extract, 1.5g
- Pinch of salt
- Water or unsweetened almond milk, optional, add 5g only if the mixed filling is too thick; add up to 5g more only if still needed

### Strawberry Sauce

- Strawberries, 40g, chopped
- Monk fruit sweetener, 3g
- Water, 3g

## Test Method

1. Make the sauce first. Simmer strawberries, 3g monk fruit, and 3g water over medium-low heat for 4-5 minutes, mashing lightly.
2. Transfer the sauce to a clean container and cool completely.
3. Mix the crushed ladyfingers with melted butter and 4g water or almond milk. Add the remaining 2g only if it will not hold together like damp sand.
4. Press the crust firmly and evenly into the bottom of one dessert container.
5. Whisk the Philadelphia filling and Greek yogurt together until completely smooth.
6. Add protein powder, 14g monk fruit, vanilla, and salt. Whisk for 60 seconds.
7. Taste the filling before adding liquid. If it is too thick, add 5g water or almond milk. Add no more than 10g total and record the actual amount.
8. Spread the filling evenly over the crust.
9. Spoon the fully cooled strawberry sauce over the filling.
10. Seal and refrigerate for at least 3 hours before judging the final texture.

## Working Macro Estimate

- Approximately 314 calories

Optional water adds no macros. Unsweetened almond milk may add a negligible amount depending on the product and quantity used. Final customer values should be calculated from the exact protein and liquid used in the successful test.

## Larger Strawberry Topping Batch For Production

- Strawberries, 500g, hulled and chopped
- Sugar or honey, start with 40g
- Lemon juice, 15g
- Pinch of salt
- Optional cornstarch, 5g mixed with 10g cold water, only if the cooled sauce is too loose

## Method

1. Add strawberries, sugar or honey, lemon, and salt to a saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook until the berries break down and the sauce thickens.
3. Mash for a textured topping or blend briefly for a smoother topping.
4. Add the cornstarch slurry only if needed, then simmer one additional minute.
5. Cool completely before adding to cheesecake cups.
6. Weigh the cooled finished yield.

Start with 35g topping per cheesecake cup. Adjust by appearance and taste, then use that exact amount for the final working macros.

Only scale the larger topping batch after the one-cup sauce is approved.

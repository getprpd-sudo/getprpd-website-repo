# Lotus Biscoff Protein Cheesecake Cup

Status: Controlled reduced-calorie first-test recipe for the July 25, 2026 menu. Not production approved until one cup passes the taste, yield, and refrigerated-hold checks below.

## Target

- One dessert cup
- Approximately 395 calories
- Approximately 40g protein
- Strong Biscoff identity without the butter-heavy crust and oversized cookie-butter portion used by conventional cheesecake

## Ingredients Per Cup

### Cheesecake filling

- 113g H-E-B fat-free cottage cheese
- 100g FAGE Total 0% Greek yogurt
- 19.5g Premier vanilla protein powder
- 10g Lotus Biscoff cookie butter
- 3g powdered sugar
- 2g vanilla extract
- 0.2g salt
- Up to 5g of the measured Fairlife milk below, only if needed for blending

### Crumb layer and finish

- 2 Lotus Biscoff cookies
- 10g Fairlife fat-free milk total, divided as needed between the crumbs and filling
- 5g Lotus Biscoff cookie butter for the top swirl

## Method

1. Crush both cookies finely. Reserve a small pinch of crumbs for the top.
2. Add enough of the measured Fairlife milk to the remaining crumbs to make a damp, pressable mixture. Press it firmly into the cup. Do not add butter.
3. Blend the cottage cheese until completely smooth.
4. Add FAGE, Premier powder, 10g Biscoff spread, powdered sugar, vanilla, salt, and any unused measured milk. Blend only until smooth.
5. Taste the filling before assembly. Do not add unmeasured honey, butter, spread, cookies, or sugar; record any approved adjustment because it changes the label.
6. Spread the filling over the crumb layer.
7. Warm the remaining 5g Biscoff spread briefly and swirl it over the top. Finish with the reserved crumbs.
8. Seal and refrigerate for at least three hours before evaluating.

## Why This Build Changed

The superseded 580-calorie draft used 32g cookie butter, three cookies, 7g butter, and honey. Conventional Biscoff cheesecake uses a butter-cookie crust, full-fat cream cheese or cream, and substantial cookie butter. Lower-calorie high-protein recipes keep the recognizable cookie and spread flavor but use a lean dairy base and tightly controlled toppings.

This PRPD build retains the full cottage-cheese/FAGE base and one physical Premier scoop while reducing the calorie-dense flavor components. The dessert remains a full cup rather than becoming a very small cheesecake bite.

## Approval Checks

- Filling is completely smooth, not grainy.
- Biscoff flavor is obvious in both the filling and top swirl.
- Two-cookie crumb layer covers the cup base adequately.
- Sweetness is sufficient without extra honey or cookie butter.
- Cup remains thick and presentable after three refrigerated days.
- One batch yields the planned number of full cups without shortage.
- Confirm the purchased Biscoff cookie and spread labels before printing customer labels.

After the test passes, record any measured adjustment and regenerate nutrition, cost, production, grocery, and label data.

The old rice-based record does not control this wrap build. The fresh test showed that 180g raw beef tastes and fits but requires moving the ends toward the center for closure. The Lean production decision remains 170g raw beef for one full shawarma.

The Bulk presentation remains one and one-half complete shawarmas, cut into three halves. Use 210g raw seekh mixture total and distribute it evenly so each half contains approximately 70g raw-equivalent filling. This preserves the three-piece presentation while reducing the working build to approximately 770 calories and 56g protein. Confirm closure, even filling, and reheated texture once before label lock.

## Working Macro Range

Using a standard 90/10 beef estimate, approximately 200 calories of bread, vegetables, and a controlled 30g total sauce serving:

- Lean: approximately 640-690 calories / 42-46g protein
- Bulk: approximately 770 calories / 56g protein, pending actual sauce and beef yield

These ranges are intentionally broad. The actual beef package and weekly sauces can move the result. The existing website values of 505/48 Lean and 605/60 Bulk should be treated as outdated rice-dish estimates and replaced after the wrap test.

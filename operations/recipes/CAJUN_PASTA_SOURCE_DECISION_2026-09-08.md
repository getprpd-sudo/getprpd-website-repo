## Original four-serving source reference, before PRPD portion changes

Ingredients:

- Boneless, skinless thigh meat: 1 lb, about 454 g; cut into 1.5-2 cm cubes.
- Dry penne: 8 oz, about 227 g.
- Fire-roasted diced tomatoes, with juice: 15 oz, about 425 g.
- Water: 2 US cups, about 475 mL, replacing all broth.
- Regular cream cheese: 2 oz, about 57 g.
- One diced yellow onion; three sliced green onions.
- Olive oil and butter: 1 tablespoon each.
- Spice mix: smoked paprika 2 teaspoons; oregano and thyme 1 teaspoon each; garlic powder and onion powder 1/2 teaspoon each; cayenne, black pepper and salt 1/4 teaspoon each.

Method:

1. Coat thigh cubes with the mixed seasonings.
2. Brown them in the oil and butter. Add yellow onion and soften, loosening the browned bits.
3. Add dry pasta, tomatoes with juice, and water. Cover, bring to a boil, then simmer gently, stirring regularly, until pasta is tender and sauce thickens.
4. Check chicken reaches 165 F. Melt cream cheese into the sauce and finish with green onions.

PRPD adaptation notes: water adds no broth flavor or sodium. Brown the chicken and onion properly; do not silently compensate with unmeasured seasonings. If liquid is insufficient before pasta is tender, add water in small measured increments and record it. Do not add extra oil or cheese without recording it. Metric equivalents above follow the US measures rather than copying the source card's rounded metric annotations.

## Source and adaptation statement

This is a PRPD adaptation, not a published-source recipe. The dish concept is supported by Food Network's salmon with creamy Alfredo sauce. Pilot 1 borrowed the lighter milk, Greek-yogurt, and reduced-fat cream-cheese direction from PRPD's Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta. The owner rejected that lighter direction and requested a tastier cream-based sauce. Pilot 2 used a large Alfredo allocation, but its estimated 845-calorie Lean meal was too high. Pilot 3 keeps real heavy cream, whole milk, regular cream cheese, butter, Parmesan, and garlic, while sharply reducing the fat-heavy ingredients and using a small measured cornstarch allocation for body. Greek yogurt remains excluded. Protein pasta and broccoli remain the sides.

- Food Network concept reference: <https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/salmon-with-creamy-alfredo-sauce-recipe-1970529>
- Food-safety reference: <https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart>

## Required decisions after pilot

1. Pilot the sauce across both salmon and pasta, with the majority mixed through the pasta and a measured finishing spoon over the salmon. Confirm that balance during the taste test.
2. Confirm flavor, salt, garlic level, sauce thickness, and fish-to-pasta balance.
3. Record drained salmon weight, skin and trim loss, cooked edible salmon yield, cooked pasta yield, broccoli yield, and finished sauce yield.
4. Perform the 24-hour and 72-hour reheat test. The richer dairy emulsion can still separate or oil out if overheated, so this remains a release gate.
5. Recalculate Lean and Bulk nutrition from actual edible yields and current package panels.
6. Cost both tiers and obtain an exact photo before menu publication.

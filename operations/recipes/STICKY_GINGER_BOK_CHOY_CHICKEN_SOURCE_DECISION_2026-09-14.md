# Chinese-Style Bok Choy Chicken source decision

Prepared September 14, 2026. Status: owner approved for the Batch 11 menu and generated into the order page, nutrition data, Grocery Builder, Cook-Day Planner and Label Studio.

## Source finding

The owner remembers cooking a successful bok choy chicken dish for Doval, but no exact formula was found in the archived recipe PDF, the current Word master, current recipe records or prior production notes. `Boy Kibble` is unrelated and is not a source for this dish.

The earlier Food Network candidate used tamari and fish sauce. The owner rejected both ingredients and asked for a more recognizable Chinese flavor base. The live dish is therefore a clearly labeled PRPD adaptation, not a claim that the Food Network recipe or the remembered Doval recipe was reproduced.

## Method and controls

1. Whisk soy sauce, hoisin, rice vinegar, honey, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, cornstarch and white pepper. Refrigerate the measured sauce.
2. Separate bok choy stems from leaves. Slice the stems and keep leaves in wide ribbons. Slice pepper and scallions.
3. Pan-sear chicken in uncrowded batches to 165 F. Remove and record cooked yield.
4. Stir-fry stems and pepper first. Add leaves only when the stems are crisp-tender.
5. Return chicken, whisk in the measured sauce and cook until glossy and fully thickened. Fold in scallions and serve with the measured rice.

There is no tamari, fish sauce, oyster sauce or shellfish ingredient. Confirm the purchased soy sauce and hoisin are halal suitable. Expected major allergens are soy and wheat; verify the physical brand labels because sesame content varies by hoisin brand.

The first production run must still record sauce yield, chicken yield, bok choy texture, finished weights and refrigerated reheat quality. Software approval does not replace that kitchen check.

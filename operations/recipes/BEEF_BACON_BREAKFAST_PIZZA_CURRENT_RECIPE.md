# Beef Bacon Breakfast Pizza — Batch 19 accepted kitchen build

**Portion:** Divide one 235 g dough batch into two separate rectangles: **100 g dough for Lean** and **125 g for Bulk**. The owner's successful test used these amounts; the other 10 g was not placed on either pizza. Shape by eye to fit the meal box. Use only H-E-B Fat Free Mozzarella Shredded Cheese for the mozzarella.

**Package fit:** The owner measured the current 32 oz meal box at approximately 8 × 5.2 × 2 inches. Use the actual box to guide the rectangle size and confirm the closed lid after baking. Both rectangles can bake together on the roughly 12 inch round stone if arranged with a small gap.

**House sauce:** Simmer canned tomatoes with garlic, olive oil, Italian seasoning and red chili flakes. Use 15 g finished sauce on each pizza. Record the oil and finished sauce-batch weights on Cook Day so incorporated oil can be counted in nutrition.

The dough totals approximately 235 g before proofing; use the 100 g/125 g portions that succeeded rather than adding the spare 10 g to Bulk. In the successful first bake, 150 g cooked eggs were left over; the owner felt about 20 g more could have gone on Bulk. The cooked egg batch's starting weight was not recorded, so **one medium egg plus 175 g whites is the current nutrition/planning basis, not a measured final egg allocation**. The owner will settle the eggs from the next prep. Use actual as-packaged bacon weight for nutrition and cost, then separately record its cooked yield. The first test used ground beef as a placeholder; customer production uses Sharifa bacon. The owner reports the bacon finish is routine and does not require a separate trial.

## Stone method

1. Place the stone on the second-lowest rack and preheat the oven to **425°F for 45–60 minutes**. Let refrigerated dough warm until pliable. Divide into 100 g Lean and 125 g Bulk. Shape two separate rectangles by eye on parchment rated for the oven temperature, checking that each will fit in the meal box. Leave a small gap between them and trim parchment close to the dough.
2. Weigh **50 g bacon for Lean** and **60 g bacon for Bulk** from the package before cooking; keep the two allocations separate. Cook all bacon before topping. Leave one cooked but still pliable slice intact for each pizza; crisp and chop the rest into bits, then drain. Do not put a raw slice on the pizza for the short final bake. Sauté peppers and onion until excess moisture is gone.
3. For the next measured prep, gently scramble **1 egg + 175 g egg whites** until just set. Weigh the finished mixture, put roughly 75–85 g cooked on Lean and 95–110 g on Bulk, and record the actual amounts. Check egg dishes reach **160°F**.
4. Top each raw dough rectangle with **15 g house sauce**, its cooked eggs, 30 g vegetables, and its cooked whole bacon slice. Use **30 g mozzarella on Lean** and **58 g on Bulk**. Keep toppings in a thin, even layer. Hold the crisp chopped bacon bits until the pizza comes out, then scatter them over the melted cheese so they stay crisp. Slice counts vary, so portion the bacon by raw package weight, not count.
5. Slide both topped rectangles and parchment onto the fully preheated stone. **Check at 8 minutes**, then continue until the underside is browned, the center is not gummy and the cheese is melted. The first test, on the second-lowest rack at 425°F, finished in **about 9–10 minutes** and had a well-browned bottom. Treat that as a trial result, not a guaranteed time for every batch.
6. Cool briefly on a rack so the bottom does not steam soft.
7. Pack each rectangle whole. Check the closed lid, packed weights, fresh taste and next-day chilled reheat texture before customer production.

## Menu nutrition basis

**Current recipe-based estimates:** approximately **450 calories / 36 g protein for Lean** and **580 calories / 51 g protein for Bulk**, before a measured correction for the house sauce oil and any unused cooked egg. The previous menu estimates of Lean 540/44 and Bulk 670/62 were based on equal 117.5 g dough portions and substantially more egg; they do not describe this accepted build. Ground beef used in the first test does not substitute for Sharifa bacon in customer-facing nutrition. Record actual cooked egg portions, sauce oil/batch yield and bacon package weights before final production labels.

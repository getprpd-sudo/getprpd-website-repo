## Historical three-cup test, superseded

## Why the Old Version Failed

The previous three-cup formula used 250g cottage cheese, 300g Fage, and 1.5 scoops of protein powder. That made the cream protein-forward rather than tiramisu-forward: too firm, too bitter, and not rich enough to taste like mascarpone cream.

This version intentionally accepts more calories and less protein. Mascarpone supplies the recognizable tiramisu flavor, while Fage, a smaller amount of blended cottage cheese, and only half a scoop of protein powder keep the dessert meaningfully higher in protein than a standard cup.

### Three-Cup Test Formula

### Cream

- Mascarpone: 90g
- Fage 0% Greek yogurt: 300g
- Cottage cheese: 90g
- Vanilla protein powder: 17.5g, approximately one-half labeled scoop
- Powdered sugar: 30g
- Vanilla extract: 5g
- Cooled strong espresso: 15g
- Fine salt: 0.5g

### Coffee and Cake Layers

- Ladyfingers: start with 90g total, 30g per cup
- Strong espresso or coffee, cooled: prepare 150g
- Granulated sugar for the coffee: 9g

The exact coffee used is the weight gained by the ladyfingers, not the full 150g prepared. Quickly dip each piece for about one second per side. Do not soak until collapsing.

### Sweet Cocoa Finish

- Unsweetened cocoa powder: 3g
- Powdered sugar: 6g

Sift together at a 1:2 cocoa-to-sugar ratio and apply a light 3g total mixture per cup. This is a PRPD taste-test adjustment to control bitterness, not a traditional tiramisu standard. If the finish is still bitter, test 1:3 before adding more sugar to the cream.

## Method

1. Blend the cottage cheese by itself until completely smooth, scraping the blender as needed.
2. Add the Fage, mascarpone, protein powder, powdered sugar, vanilla, 15g cooled espresso, and salt.
3. Blend only until smooth. Do not overmix mascarpone until loose or watery.
4. Weigh the finished cream and divide it into three equal portions.
5. Dissolve 9g sugar in the warm coffee, then cool it completely.
6. For each cup, quickly dip 30g ladyfingers. Record their weight after dipping so coffee pickup is known.
7. Build two visible cake-and-cream layers. Finish with cream.
8. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
9. Add the sweet cocoa finish shortly before serving or photography.

## Taste Decision

Approve only if all are true:

- It tastes like coffee and mascarpone before it tastes like protein powder.
- The cream is soft and spoonable, not rigid or grainy.
- The ladyfingers are moist but still form visible layers.
- The cocoa finish is chocolatey without a bitter first impression.
- It still tastes good directly from the refrigerator the next day.

If it is too firm, add 15-30g milk to the full cream batch on the next test. If it is not rich enough, replace 30g Fage with 30g additional mascarpone. Do not solve either problem by adding more protein powder.

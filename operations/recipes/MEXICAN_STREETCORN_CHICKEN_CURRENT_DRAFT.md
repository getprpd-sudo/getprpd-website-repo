# Mexican Streetcorn Chicken Bowl

Status: Kitchen test required

## Finished Dish

Plate the three components side by side rather than mixing the entire bowl:

1. Cilantro-lime rice
2. Grilled seasoned boneless skinless chicken thigh
3. Creamy Mexican street corn

Traditional elote and esquites center on corn, mayonnaise or crema, Cotija, lime, chile, garlic, and sometimes cilantro. Deconstructed versions may also use red onion and jalapeno. Do not add a generic vegetable mix; it would make the corn taste less like street corn.

## Lean Build

- Chicken thigh, 200g raw; approximately 150g cooked
- Basmati rice, 40g dry; approximately 120g cooked
- Corn, 88g
- Red onion, 10g finely diced
- Jalapeno, 4g finely diced; keep mild
- Fage 0%, 25g
- Light mayonnaise, 7g
- Cotija, 10g
- Cilantro, 3g chopped
- Lime juice, 10g
- Tajin or mild chile powder, 1g
- Garlic powder, 0.5g
- Salt to taste
- Avocado oil, start with 2g total for chicken and charring corn

## Bulk Build

- Chicken thigh, 275g raw; approximately 206g cooked
- Basmati rice, 55g dry; approximately 165g cooked
- Corn, 110g
- Red onion, 12g finely diced
- Jalapeno, 5g finely diced; keep mild
- Fage 0%, 30g
- Light mayonnaise, 7g
- Cotija, 12g
- Cilantro, 4g chopped
- Lime juice, 12g
- Tajin or mild chile powder, 1.5g
- Garlic powder, 0.5g
- Salt to taste
- Avocado oil, start with 3g total for chicken and charring corn

## Chicken Seasoning

Per 1,000g raw boneless skinless chicken thigh:

- Smoked paprika, 5g
- Cumin, 4g
- Garlic powder, 4g
- Mild chile powder, 2g
- Salt, start with 8g and adjust after tasting
- Lime juice, 30g

## Method

1. Season chicken and rest 15-30 minutes.
2. Cook over high heat until browned and safely cooked. Rest, then dice or slice.
3. Cook the rice and finish with lime and cilantro.
4. Char the corn in a hot pan with the measured oil.
5. Add onion, jalapeno, and garlic powder; cook briefly so the vegetables soften without disappearing.
6. Remove from high heat. Fold in Fage, light mayo, Cotija, lime, cilantro, Tajin, and salt.
7. Plate rice, chicken, and creamy corn in three visible sections.

## Storage

Fridge only. Keep any additional sauce separate. The corn mixture is not intended as a days 5-7 freezer meal.

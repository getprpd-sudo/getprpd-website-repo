## Controlled method

1. Keep the **20 g marinade yogurt** and **15 g chilled-finish yogurt** separate from the beginning.
2. Combine only the ingredients listed under Tikka marinade with the raw thigh. Keep refrigerated while marinating.
3. Cook the chicken to **165 F**, then cool promptly in a shallow container before wrap assembly.
4. Make the chilled yogurt-herb finish in a separate sanitized bowl. Never reuse raw-chicken marinade as the finish.
5. Dry the washed vegetables thoroughly. Place all cooked chicken, vegetables and finish into one medium tortilla. Roll tightly and hold refrigerated at 40 F or below.
6. Record cooked chicken yield and a next-day held-wrap check before treating package fit and texture as fully verified.

## Source and adaptation note

Mission Foodservice's [Tikka Glazed Chicken Wrap](https://missionfoodservice.com/recipes/tikka-glazed-chicken-wrap/) supports the yogurt, garlic, coriander, cayenne/paprika and lemon flavor direction. PRPD's version is a one-wrap Grab & Go adaptation using chicken thigh, crisp vegetables and a measured yogurt-herb finish. It intentionally excludes the source recipe's chicken tenders, rice, cashews, large tortilla and creamy tomato sauce.

Food-safety controls follow USDA guidance: poultry reaches [165 F](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart), and perishable cooked food is refrigerated within two hours in shallow containers under USDA's [four food-safety steps](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/steps-keep-food-safe).

# Loaded Cheeseburger Potato Bowl R&D

Status: **PINNED / DEFERRED**

This concept is saved for later development. It is not approved for the menu, production, purchasing, labels, nutrition, or customer communication.

## Concept

A reheatable cheeseburger-style bowl built around roasted potatoes, seasoned ground beef, cheese, and a separate cold burger sauce. The bowl format is intended to avoid the filling-distribution, dough-folding, and reheating failures seen in the retired Cheeseburger Hot Pockets.

## Development direction

- Build an original PRPD formula rather than copying a food-blogger recipe.
- Keep the hot components simple: potatoes, beef, and cheese.
- Hold any lettuce, pickles, fresh tomato, and cold sauce separately if they are included in the final version.
- Resolve the ordinary ground-beef grade and drained-yield assumptions from a physical package and cook test before calculating nutrition or cost.
- Do not publish estimated macros, ingredient weights, or pricing as validated facts.

## First controlled test

Prepare six bowls:

- 3 Lean and 3 Bulk
- One Lean and one Bulk evaluated fresh
- One Lean and one Bulk evaluated after 24 hours
- One Lean and one Bulk evaluated after 72 hours

Record:

- actual beef product, grade, package weight, and purchase price
- raw beef weight and cooked drained yield
- raw potato weight and cooked yield
- cheese, onion, sauce, and any topping weights
- final assembled weight for every bowl
- active labor time and total elapsed time
- taste, texture, moisture, reheating, and sauce performance
- packaging fit and whether cold toppings remain genuinely useful

## Release gates

The dish stays deferred until the owner approves the physical test and the following are reconciled from measured results:

1. Repeatable Lean and Bulk builds
2. Verified nutrition calculation
3. Verified cost and target margin
4. Reheat performance through 72 hours
5. Production steps suitable for the Cook-Day Planner and Grocery Builder

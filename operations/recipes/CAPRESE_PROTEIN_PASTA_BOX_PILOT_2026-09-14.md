## One-box development formula

- Barilla Protein+ Rotini, dry: 66 g
- Cannellini beans, drained and rinsed: 100 g
- H-E-B fat-free mozzarella: 28 g
- Grape or cherry tomatoes, halved and drained: 45 g
- Fresh basil, thinly sliced: 4 g
- Balsamic-yogurt dressing: 20 g, drizzled over the finished chilled box before sealing

Development dressing for one box:

- Plain nonfat Greek yogurt: 14 g
- Balsamic vinegar: 5 g
- Garlic powder: 0.3 g
- Fine salt: 0.3 g
- Black pepper: 0.2 g
- Water only if needed for pourability: up to 0.2 g, recorded exactly

## Test method

1. Cook the rotini to the package's firm pasta-salad texture. Drain, cool rapidly and record cooked yield.
2. Rinse and drain the beans thoroughly. Halve the tomatoes and let cut liquid drain so the box does not become watery.
3. Combine cooled pasta, beans, mozzarella and tomatoes. Fold in basil only after every component is cold.
4. Drizzle the measured 20 g dressing evenly over the top only after every component is cold. Do not pack a separate cup.
5. Pack one 12-ounce box, weigh it, close it, stack another box on top and confirm the lid does not bow or contact the food.
6. Hold at 41 F or below. Inspect at 24, 48 and 72 hours for tomato weeping, basil darkening, dry pasta, watery beans, dressing pooling or off aroma.
7. Taste the dressed box at 24 and 72 hours. The pasta must remain seasoned and tender without becoming oily or mushy.

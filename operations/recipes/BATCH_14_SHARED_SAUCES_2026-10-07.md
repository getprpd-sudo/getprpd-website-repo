## 1. PRPD Honey Barbecue Mustard

This is a PRPD adaptation, not a copied source recipe. Published Carolina-style mustard barbecue recipes consistently use yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, honey, and dry seasonings. Food Network uses mustard, vinegar, honey, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. A Couple Cooks uses mustard, honey, apple cider vinegar, tomato paste, soy sauce, and hot sauce. PRPD replaces the tomato/Worcestershire/soy direction with measured G Hughes sugar-free barbecue sauce, then keeps the mustard, honey, vinegar, and spice structure.

Sources:

- [Food Network, Tangy Mustard BBQ Sauce](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/tangy-mustard-bbq-sauce-south-carolina-style-3339225)
- [A Couple Cooks, Mustard BBQ Sauce](https://www.acouplecooks.com/mustard-bbq-sauce/)

### Method and test gate

1. Tare a sanitized bowl. Whisk mustard, barbecue sauce, honey, and vinegar until uniform.
2. Whisk in garlic powder, smoked paprika, black pepper, and salt. Scrape the bowl and record total input.
3. Rest refrigerated for at least 30 minutes, then taste cold with the actual chicken or steak application.
4. Record sweetness, mustard sharpness, smoke, acidity, salt, viscosity, and whether it clings after refrigeration.
5. Weigh finished sauce and bowl residue. For customer production, scale the same ratio to the locked planner quantity and fill the exact displayed 30 g or 45 g cups.

Do not free-pour an adjustment into the production batch. If the pilot needs a change, record the exact grams and update the controlled ratio before production.

## 2. PRPD Garlic Herb Yogurt

This is the current PRPD garlic-yogurt family with fresh herbs and no jalapeno. The 45 g base formula is the controlled source for both 30 g side cups and the 25 g incorporated Chicken Tikka Snack Wrap allocation.

### Method and test gate

1. Tare a sanitized cold bowl. Whisk yogurt and lemon juice smooth.
2. Fold in garlic, cilantro, parsley, cumin, salt, and water. Record total input.
3. Rest covered and refrigerated for at least 30 minutes. Stir again before tasting and filling.
4. Record garlic strength, acidity, salt, herb freshness, viscosity, and separation after 24, 48, and 72 hours at 41 F or below.
5. Weigh finished sauce and bowl residue. Scale the unchanged ratio to the locked planner quantity. Fill 30 g cups by net weight and reserve the displayed incorporated amount separately.

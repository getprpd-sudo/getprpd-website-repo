## Controlled Oven Method

1. Form every meatball at 25-26g total raw mixture.
2. Arrange in one layer on parchment-lined sheet pans without touching.
3. Bake at 400 F. Start checking at 12 minutes, rotate pans if needed, and continue only until representative centers reach 160 F. Temperature, not color or a fixed bake time, controls release.
4. Record raw count, endpoint temperature, cooked count, cooked total weight, and any damaged pieces.
5. The current nutrition model adds no cooking oil. Record any measured oil used; a light unmeasured spray is not permission to add liquid oil.

Batch 4 has nine Lean servings and no Bulk servings. At eight meatballs per Lean meal, the exact requirement is 72 pieces. The July 30 formed yield was 72 pieces at 25-26g each, so there is no spare tasting or damage piece. Use the pooled plain beef reserve only if a measured replacement is actually required.

Keep the formed meatballs covered at 41 F or below on Thursday and bake them Friday. Cooking Thursday is safe only with documented rapid cooling, but it starts the cooked-food refrigeration clock a day early and does not fit the current Saturday-delivery storage model.

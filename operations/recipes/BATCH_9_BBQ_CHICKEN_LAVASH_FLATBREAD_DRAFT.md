# Batch 9 BBQ Chicken Lavash Flatbread Draft

Status: controlled kitchen-test recipe; not approved for customer publication or final labels

## Product decision

Use one full Joseph's Flax, Oat Bran & Whole Wheat Lavash per order. Cut the sheet crosswise into two rectangles before topping, then cut each finished rectangle diagonally. The customer receives four wedges total.

Do not use two Stonefire mini naan for the controlled version. Two mini naan contribute about 290 calories before chicken, cheese, or sauce. One complete Joseph's lavash contributes 120 calories, 12 g protein, 16 g carbohydrate, 4 g fiber, and 3 g fat based on the current manufacturer panel of 60 calories and 6 g protein per half-sheet.

The physical purchased package remains authoritative. Confirm that the package panel matches before nutrition or labels are published.

## PRPD smoky honey-lime BBQ finish

This is the complete sauce for one order; it is incorporated into the flatbread and does not receive an additional customer sauce cup.

| Ingredient | Exact amount |
|---|---:|
| G Hughes sugar-free BBQ sauce | 25 g |
| Honey | 4 g |
| Lime juice | 5 g |
| **Finished target** | **34 g** |

Whisk until uniform. Toss the chopped cooked chicken with 14 g of the finish. Spread the remaining 20 g thinly over the two lavash rectangles. Do not increase the sauce without recalculating nutrition and completing another refrigerated texture test.

## Test production method

1. Heat the oven to 400 F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Trim the 150 g raw chicken thigh, then coat it with 1 g oil, 0.5 g salt, 0.5 g smoked paprika, 0.5 g garlic powder, and 0.25 g black pepper.
3. Cook the chicken to 165 F. Record the finished cooked weight. The planning target is 112.5 g; use the complete cooked yield from this exact 150 g raw test rather than discarding food merely to force the estimate.
4. Rest briefly, then chop the chicken small enough to stay on a thin flatbread.
5. Cut the lavash crosswise into two equal rectangles. Pre-crisp them on the lined pan for 2-3 minutes. The surface should feel dry and supported, but the edges should not become dark.
6. Whisk the 34 g smoky honey-lime BBQ finish. Toss the chicken with 14 g. Spread 10 g over each lavash rectangle.
7. Divide the chicken, 35 g mozzarella, and 20 g thin red onion evenly across both rectangles. Keep all toppings in one thin, even layer so the lavash does not become wet or overloaded.
8. Bake for approximately 5-7 minutes, watching continuously near the end, until the cheese melts and the edges are crisp. The exact test result controls the final time.
9. Move the flatbreads to a cooling rack immediately. Add 2 g cilantro. Rest until the cheese sets, then cut each rectangle diagonally to produce four wedges.
10. For customer production, rapid-cool before sealing. Pack four wedges in one standard meal container. If the wedges overlap, place parchment between layers so cheese does not bond them together.

## Required approval tests

1. Confirm one full lavash, four wedges, and the standard container look sufficiently full.
2. Confirm the 34 g sauce amount flavors the chicken and base without creating a wet center.
3. Confirm fat-free mozzarella melts acceptably at the controlled amount.
4. Photograph the fresh product and the packed product.
5. Refrigerate one complete packed order and test at 24 and 72 hours.
6. Reheat the held product in the microwave and in an air fryer or oven. Record customer instructions that produce the best acceptable result.
7. Record raw chicken, cooked chicken, finished sauce, finished flatbread, and leftover weights.

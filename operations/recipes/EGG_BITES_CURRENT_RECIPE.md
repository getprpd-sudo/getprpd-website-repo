## Required Tray Calibration

1. Keep the portion decision at four Lean bites and six Bulk bites until taste and satiety indicate otherwise.
2. Weigh the empty mixing bowl and the complete finished batter.
3. Portion one fixed gram weight into each of 24 wells; record grams per well and fill level.
4. Weigh any leftover batter instead of repurposing it into another recipe.
5. Record the number and average weight of baked bites.
6. Weigh salt and every seasoning separately. Confirm whether the July 18 report of 2.5 to 3 teaspoons referred to salt alone or the combined seasoning blend.
7. Record pan spray and hash oil separately.
8. Recalculate the recipe and labels from the controlled tray rather than reducing the old formula by guesswork.

## Next 24-Well Calibration Batch - 50% Test

This is a controlled test formula, not a customer-label formula. It is based on the July 18 observation that the full formula filled 24 wells to about 85% and still left batter, while the intended bite should fill roughly half a well.

- 9 whole eggs
- 265g liquid egg whites
- 226g fat-free cottage cheese
- 113g fat-free mozzarella
- 75g spinach
- 63g tomato
- 38g jalapeno
- 50% of the previously used onion weight; the onion must be weighed this time
- Start with 50% of the July 18 seasoning quantities, then taste a safely cooked tester before adjusting

Blend the wet base, fold in vegetables and cheese, then weigh the complete batter. Divide the batter weight by 24 and portion that gram amount into every well. The target is approximately 50% to 60% well fill with no meaningful leftover batter. Record total batter, grams per well, baked count, and average finished bite weight.

If this batch underfills the wells, increase the next formula from the measured batter requirement rather than returning to the full previous formula.

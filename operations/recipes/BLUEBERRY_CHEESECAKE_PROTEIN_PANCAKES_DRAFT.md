# Blueberry Cheesecake Protein Pancakes

Status: Controlled first-test recipe for the July 25, 2026 menu. Not production approved and not ready for customer labels.

## Customer Build

| Tier | Pancakes | Toppings |
|---|---:|---|
| Lean | 3 medium | Blueberry compote, cheesecake-yogurt topping, sugar-free maple syrup |
| Bulk | 4 medium | Larger compote and cheesecake-yogurt topping, sugar-free maple syrup |

## Lean First Test

### Pancake batter

- 45g all-purpose flour
- 9.75g Premier vanilla protein powder
- 3g baking powder
- 1 large whole egg
- 80g liquid egg whites
- 60g FAGE 0% Greek yogurt
- 60g Fairlife fat-free milk
- 2g vanilla extract
- 0.5g cinnamon
- 0.5g salt
- 2g retained cooking oil or measured spray equivalent

### Blueberry compote

- 80g blueberries
- 4g powdered sugar
- Small measured splash of water only if needed; record the amount

### Cheesecake-yogurt topping and syrup

- 20g Philadelphia no-bake cheesecake filling
- 40g FAGE 0% Greek yogurt
- 30g Great Value sugar-free maple syrup

The compote and cheesecake topping may share one side cup. Pack syrup in a second cup so the pancakes do not become soggy.

## Bulk First Test

### Pancake batter

- 60g all-purpose flour
- 14.625g Premier vanilla protein powder
- 4g baking powder
- 1 large whole egg
- 120g liquid egg whites
- 70g FAGE 0% Greek yogurt
- 75g Fairlife fat-free milk
- 2.5g vanilla extract
- 0.75g cinnamon
- 0.75g salt
- 3g retained cooking oil or measured spray equivalent

### Blueberry compote

- 100g blueberries
- 4g powdered sugar
- Small measured splash of water only if needed; record the amount

### Cheesecake-yogurt topping and syrup

- 25g Philadelphia no-bake cheesecake filling
- 50g FAGE 0% Greek yogurt
- 30g Great Value sugar-free maple syrup

## Method

1. Simmer the blueberries and powdered sugar until lightly thickened. Cool completely and record the finished compote weight.
2. Whisk the cheesecake filling and topping portion of Greek yogurt until smooth. Keep refrigerated.
3. Whisk the dry batter ingredients separately.
4. Blend or whisk the egg, egg whites, batter yogurt, milk, and vanilla until smooth.
5. Fold wet into dry only until combined. Rest five minutes and record the final batter weight.
6. Cook measured portions on a controlled griddle. Record griddle temperature, batter grams per pancake, pancake count, and finished weight.
7. Cool before packing. Keep both topping components separate from the pancakes.

## Approval Checks

- Lean yields exactly three presentable pancakes; Bulk yields four.
- Pancakes are fluffy rather than rubbery, chalky, or dry.
- Protein-powder flavor is not obvious.
- Cheesecake topping tastes like cheesecake rather than plain yogurt.
- Pancakes reheat successfully after three refrigerated days.
- Compote and topping do not leak or make the pancakes soggy.
- Record the exact purchased flour, blueberries, and cheesecake-filling labels before final label generation.

After the test passes, replace the draft amounts with observed yields and regenerate nutrition, costing, production, and label data.

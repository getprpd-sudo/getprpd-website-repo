## Library Control Rule

Being listed here means PRPD owns a draft or historical recipe for the dish. It does not mean the recipe is ready for production, current pricing, verified macros, or customer labels. Each entry still needs a controlled recipe record and one of these statuses:

- Draft
- Kitchen Tested
- Production Approved

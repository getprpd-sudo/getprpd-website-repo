# Current Sauce-Cup Classification

Current as of October 19, 2026. This classification uses `operations/nutrition/current_recipe_registry.py` as the current production source and audits the older structured and uncontrolled inventory separately below. Batch-specific records remain evidence of what was produced at the time, but they do not override this current policy for future batches.

## Owner policy

- There is no automatic free cup with every meal. Each customer order may select up to **3 free optional cups total**, regardless of meal count.
- Recipe-required cups are part of the meal build and meal price.
- Optional sauce cups are order-level selections. The proposed launch choices are PRPD Sweet Heat and PRPD Garlic Herb Yogurt, pending final menu approval. Cups selected after the three-free order allowance cost $1.00 each.
- Produce the exact recipe-required count plus every selected order-level cup, both free and paid. Do not assign cups by meal and do not add a blanket overage multiplier.
- Caprese dressing is incorporated into the food as a measured 20 g drizzle. It is not a cup.
- Owner-confirmed name boundary, October 19: the instruction that strawberry cheesecake receives sauce cups means **Strawberry Cheesecake Protein Pancakes**, not the baked dessert. Preserve the pancakes' strawberry-cheesecake topping cup and sugar-free maple syrup cup. **Baked Strawberry-Lemon Protein Cheesecake** has no added cup, and the older **Strawberry Cheesecake** cup format remains retired. This clarification closes the earlier product-name question.

## Production equation

`cups to fill = recipe-required cups from sold meals + selected free order-level cups + selected paid order-level cups`

The three free selections are per customer order, not per meal. Transfer allowance is calculated as sauce weight, not extra filled customer cups. Home meals and speculative reserves remain excluded unless deliberately recorded.

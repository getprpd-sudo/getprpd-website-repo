# Mexican Streetcorn Chicken Bowl Test Plan

Status: Draft. The website currently displays target macros, but those values are not Production Approved until the formula, cooked yields, and plated weights below are tested.

## Current Product Direction

- Grilled halal chicken
- Rice
- Charred corn
- Jalapeno crema
- Cotija cheese
- Fresh lime
- Lean target currently displayed: 530 calories, 54 g protein, 44 g carbs, 10 g fat
- Bulk target currently displayed: 660 calories, 73 g protein, 58 g carbs, 12 g fat

These targets are a starting point, not evidence. Final macros must come from the exact tested brands and finished portion weights.

## Test Goals

- Define a repeatable chicken marinade and cooked yield.
- Keep the corn visibly charred without becoming watery after refrigeration.
- Define a crema that reheats or remains cold without splitting.
- Balance lime, jalapeno, cotija, and salt after a 24-hour chill.
- Approve exact Lean and Bulk component weights.
- Confirm the bowl remains appealing after normal refrigerated storage and reheating.

## Procedure

1. Photograph and record every ingredient nutrition panel and exact brand.
2. Weigh chicken before trimming, after trimming/marinating, and after cooking/resting.
3. Weigh dry rice and finished cooked rice; record the equipment and water ratio.
4. Weigh corn before and after charring. Record whether it was frozen, canned, or fresh and whether it was drained.
5. Prepare crema as its own weighed sub-recipe and record total yield.
6. Assemble one Lean and one Bulk bowl strictly by the proposed gram weights.
7. Weigh each finished bowl without the container.
8. Evaluate one bowl fresh, one after 24 hours refrigerated, and one after the intended storage period.
9. Reheat using the customer-facing method. Score chicken moisture, rice texture, corn texture, crema stability, salt, acidity, and appearance.
10. Adjust the controlled formula, repeat once, and approve only when the repeated batch lands within the portion tolerance.

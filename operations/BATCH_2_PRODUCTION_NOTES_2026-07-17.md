### Rice Shortage

- Rice was short again; the Mexican Streetcorn Chicken Bowls did not receive their planned rice component.
- The 98-item active packing manifest required 33 rice meals at a 120g cooked target: 3,960g cooked rice total.
- Recipe-level dry rice for those active meals was 1,320g. The planner displayed a 3.0x working yield, not a 4.0x yield.
- USDA's Food Buying Guide reports roughly 2.75 to 3 cups cooked per cup dry for common long- and medium-grain rice. Volume yield is not a guaranteed weight yield for PRPD's brand and method.
- Root cause cannot be closed without the actual dry rice started, actual cooked rice yield, and the amount plated into each dish pan.
- Temporary next-run control: calculate dry rice from the cooked requirement using a conservative 2.75x working yield and a 15% dry-rice planning allowance. For a 3,960g cooked target, this stages approximately 1,656g dry rice. Replace this temporary rule after one complete measured batch.

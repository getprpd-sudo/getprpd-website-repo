# Private Records Policy

`operations/private-records/` is local-only and ignored by Git.

It currently contains:

- Original Walmart receipt images for July 16 and July 23.
- Transcribed Restaurant Depot, Oasis, and Costco receipt details.
- PRPD LLC formation documents copied from the Downloads folder.
- `customers/` for customer-specific correspondence or supporting records that are not already maintained in the live Google Sheet.
- `testimonials/consents/` and `testimonials/submissions/` for signed permissions and raw customer media.
- `partnerships/zee-hamoda/agreements/`, `approvals/`, and `analytics/` for signatures, content approvals, restrictions, and private performance screenshots.
- `referrals/exports/` for any customer-level referral ledger exports.

Do not move these files into a tracked or publicly deployed folder. Back them up only to an approved private account. The live Google Sheet and production APIs remain the source of truth for customers, orders, payments, delivery information, and referral activity; do not create competing customer ledgers here. Bank statements and credentials are deliberately excluded.

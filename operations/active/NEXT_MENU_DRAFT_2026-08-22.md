## Approved Recipe Decisions

- Cajun Garlic Salmon uses wild pink salmon with lemon-herb quinoa and roasted green beans. The new side avoids repeating the menu's potatoes, basmati rice, broccoli, corn, or pasta.
- Salmon is roasted with a Cajun-garlic seasoning; it is not marketed as blackened because the approved production method does not require stovetop blackening.
- Future tilapia rotation is named **Lemon Pepper Tilapia**, keeping the fish concepts distinct. Tilapia is not part of Batch 7.
- Butter Chicken remains broth-free.
- Sweet Heat belongs only to the Beef Bacon Breakfast Sandwich and Power Bowl in this rotation.
- Chicken Caesar Crunch Box keeps its dressing and croutons separate for texture.
- Only exact, approved dish photos are attached to public menu cards. A polished PRPD placeholder is used instead of a misleading photo when no exact image exists.

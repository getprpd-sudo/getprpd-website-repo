# PRPD DFW Local Discovery

Updated: July 22, 2026

## Recommendation

Start with independent fitness operators because the owner or head coach can make a decision quickly. Offer one small tasting, one tracked partner code, and one specific activation such as a member sampling table, challenge-week meal support, or preorder pickup. Do not lead with a passive flyer drop.

## Offer Structure

- A small tasting first; no broad free-meal commitment.
- Unique percentage code with a maximum discount so margin is controlled.
- Unique UTM link or QR code tied to the same partner.
- Review after the first four attributed orders before expanding the offer.
- Track booked revenue, collected revenue, discount, food cost, and repeat purchase separately.

## Compliance

Creators receiving free food, discounts, or payment must clearly and conspicuously disclose the relationship. Google reviews must not be incentivized. A service-area Google Business Profile should not expose a residential address.

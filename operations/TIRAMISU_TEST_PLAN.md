# High Protein Tiramisu Test Plan

Status: Draft. Do not publish final macros or print final labels from the current recipe until this test is complete.

## Problem to Resolve

The current recipe says 15-18 ladyfingers for three cups, or 5-6 per cup. Its assembly step says five ladyfingers in each of two layers per cup, which would require 30 ladyfingers. The final formula must use grams, not an ambiguous count.

## Test Goal

Produce a cup that:

- fits the real PRPD container with clean headspace;
- has two distinct cake/cream layers;
- is moist after an overnight chill without becoming watery;
- has no grainy cottage-cheese texture;
- uses a repeatable finished weight;
- has macros calculated from the final tested brands and weights.

## Before Starting

- [ ] Record container size and empty container weight.
- [ ] Photograph every ingredient nutrition label.
- [ ] Record exact cottage cheese, Fage, protein powder, honey, ladyfinger, coffee, and cocoa brands.
- [ ] Weigh ten dry ladyfingers together, then calculate average grams per piece.
- [ ] Weigh the complete prepared coffee.

## Controlled Base Formula

Use the current cream base as the starting point, but weigh every addition:

- Cottage cheese: 250 g
- Fage: 300 g
- Vanilla protein powder: actual grams for 1.5 labeled scoops
- Honey: weigh the actual three tablespoons in grams
- Vanilla: weigh or measure consistently

Do not choose the final ladyfinger quantity before measuring the cup and cream yield.

## Test Procedure

1. Blend cottage cheese alone until visually smooth. Record blend time.
2. Add the remaining cream ingredients. Record total finished cream weight.
3. Divide cream into equal target portions by weight.
4. Build three cups with different measured ladyfinger weights that fit two layers. Record the dry weight used in each cup.
5. Weigh each ladyfinger portion before and immediately after dipping. The difference is coffee pickup.
6. Assemble each cup and record finished net weight.
7. Chill covered for at least three hours and evaluate again after an overnight chill.
8. Score each cup for coffee flavor, sweetness, structure, creaminess, sogginess, and portion appearance.
9. Select the best version and repeat it once before approving the recipe.

## Test 1 - French Toast, One Lean Meal

Fresh taste passed on July 14. The test formula coated four slices completely plus one partial slice, so it is now treated as a four-slice custard unit. Increase its brown sugar to 12g and complete the refrigerated reheat check before final approval.

### Custard

- Bread: 3 slices; record brand, grams, and label macros
- Whole egg: 1 large
- Egg whites: 75g
- Cottage cheese: 75g
- Milk: 40g
- Vanilla protein powder: 8.7g, approximately one-quarter labeled scoop
- Brown sugar: 12g for the next version; the approved fresh test used 6g but needed more sweetness
- Vanilla extract: 2.5g, approximately 1/2 teaspoon
- Ground cinnamon: 1.3g, approximately 1/2 teaspoon
- Fine salt: small pinch
- Butter for cooking: start with 5g and record actual use

### Packed Sides

- Strawberries: 50g
- Banana: 40g
- Whipped cream: 10g, packed separately

### Method and Decision

1. Blend the egg, egg whites, cottage cheese, milk, protein powder, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt until completely smooth.
2. Dip the bread evenly without leaving it in the custard long enough to break.
3. Cook in the measured butter over medium heat until browned and the center is custardy rather than wet.
4. Taste fresh, then refrigerate one portion and reheat it before approval.

Pass only if it tastes like good French toast with no chalky protein flavor, rubbery egg-white texture, or dry center.

## Test 2 - Mexican Streetcorn Chicken Bowl, One Lean Meal

Deferred because corn was not available on July 14. Chicken flavor is already known, but the street-corn component remains unverified. If ordered, make and taste a small portion of the corn mixture before scaling the production batch.

### Chicken and Rice

- Boneless skinless chicken thigh: 200g raw, target approximately 150g cooked
- Basmati rice: 40g dry, target approximately 120g cooked
- Avocado oil: 2g total for the chicken and corn
- Smoked paprika: 1g
- Cumin: 0.8g
- Garlic powder: 0.8g
- Mild chile powder: 0.4g
- Salt: 1.6g starting point
- Lime juice for chicken: 6g

### Street Corn

- Corn: 88g
- Red onion: 10g, finely diced
- Mild jalapeno: 4g, finely diced
- Fage 0%: 25g
- Light mayonnaise: 7g
- Cotija: 10g
- Cilantro: 3g
- Lime juice: 10g
- Tajin or mild chile powder: 1g
- Garlic powder: 0.5g
- Salt: adjust after mixing

### Method and Decision

1. Season the chicken and rest for 15-30 minutes. Cook, rest, and slice.
2. Cook the rice and finish it lightly with lime and cilantro.
3. Char the corn using part of the measured oil.
4. Briefly soften the onion and jalapeno with the corn.
5. Remove from high heat before folding in Fage, mayo, Cotija, lime, cilantro, Tajin, garlic, and salt.
6. Plate the rice, chicken, and street corn in three visible sections.

Pass only if the corn tastes like elote rather than generic creamy vegetables, the chicken is not dry, and all three sections still taste balanced together.

## Fit Test - Beef Seekh Kabab Shawarma

The regular shawarma flavor is already approved. Test construction only.

### Lean

- One approximately 200-calorie wrap
- 170g raw 90/10 seekh mixture
- Lettuce: 25g
- Onion: 15g
- Pickled turnip/cucumber/onion mixture: 30g, drained well
- Weekly sauce 1: 15g
- Weekly sauce 2: 15g

### Bulk - Recommended Format

- Use one and one-half complete shawarmas, cut into three halves.
- One full bread is 240 calories; 1.5 breads are 360 calories before filling.
- Use 210g raw seekh mixture total, distributed evenly across 1.5 breads so each of the three halves contains approximately 70g raw-equivalent filling.
- The three pieces fit the current container.
- Check ingredient cost against the current $2 Bulk upgrade.

Do not force all Bulk meat into one wrap. Three shawarma halves give the extra meat a proper bread, vegetable, pickle, and sauce structure while still looking like a deliberate single meal.

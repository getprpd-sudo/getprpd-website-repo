# PRPD Active Menu Recipe Audit

Updated: July 14, 2026

This audit compares the latest recipe PDF with the live order configuration. It is a practical working list for this week's menu. Taste comes first; calories and protein are honest working estimates calculated after the recipe and portion are selected.

## Status Key

- **Use current recipe:** the existing recipe is suitable for this week; record actual quantities during cook day.
- **Taste test:** cook the proposed version before locking its customer-facing values.
- **Rebuild:** the historical record does not represent the meal now being sold.
- **Record recipe:** PRPD already has a dish concept, but the actual formula must be written down.

## Immediate Decisions

1. Retire Cut/Maintain/Build from active recipes. Use Lean and Bulk only.
2. Use 90/10 ground beef for current beef recipes.
3. Do not reuse the rice-based Beef Seekh macros for the shawarma.
4. Keep every recipe in one of three states: Draft, Kitchen Tested, or Current Recipe.
5. Publish calories and protein as rounded working estimates based on the Current Recipe.
6. Do not weaken taste merely to preserve an old macro target.
7. Use the shared side system in `WEEKLY_SIDE_SYSTEM.md`; do not invent a separate side prep for every dish.

## Standardization Order

1. Egg Bites: confirm the 4/6-bite builds in one real batch.

3. Beef Seekh Kabab Shawarma: identify the bread and test the Bulk extra-seekh format.

7. Capture actual quantities for the recipes already suitable for this week.
